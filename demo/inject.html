Argus demo controls

Drag this button to your bookmarks bar:

◆ Demo Controls

Then open the cockpit at http://localhost:4010 and click the bookmark. A small draggable, minimizable control panel appears in the corner. It only calls the starter kit's control API — it does not modify the cockpit.

Backup (if the bookmark bar is hidden): open the cockpit, press F12 → Console, paste the contents of demo/control_mini.js, Enter.
